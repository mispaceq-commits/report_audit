#1
High Priority Conversion Best Suggestion

Upgrade Your Hero Section

Clarity + Conversion Focus

Problem: Your hero is vague — it doesn't immediately answer the visitor's core question: "What's in it for me?"

Fix: Replace the existing hero with a clear, benefit-driven headline, a supporting subheadline, and a single dominant CTA.

Example direction
  • Headline: “Get [Primary Result] Without [Big Pain Point]”
  • Subheadline: “Join [audience] who use [product/service] to [specific outcome].”
  • CTA: “Start [Desired Action] →”
Expected lift
+15–30%
Effort
~6 hrs
Decision
< 3s
to stay or leave
Why this matters

Visitors decide in under three seconds whether to stay or leave. A clear, outcome-driven hero significantly lifts engagement and reduces bounce rate.

#2
Quick Win Content Lead Capture

Add an Exit-Intent Pop-Up

Capture lost conversions

Problem: Users who leave your site today are lost permanently — there is no recovery mechanism in place.

Fix: Implement an exit-intent pop-up offering a compelling reason to stay — or to exchange contact info.

Offer ideas
  • “Wait! Get a Free Case Review”
  • “Download the Free Settlement Guide Before You Go”
Example copy
  • H: “Don't Miss This.”
  • Body: “Before you leave, grab [specific benefit].”
  • CTA: “Get It Now”
Expected lift
+5–15%
Effort
~3 hrs
Recovers
5–15% of
abandoners
Why this matters

Exit-intent pop-ups recover 5–15% of abandoning visitors, turning lost traffic into leads or customers — with no extra ad spend.

#3
Med Effort Technical Funnel

Remove Friction from Your Primary CTA

Simplify the path to conversion

Problem: Your current funnel introduces unnecessary friction — too many steps, unclear next action, or competing CTAs.

Fix: Streamline the user journey so there is one clear next step at every stage.

Quick wins
  • · Reduce form fields to essentials
  • · One primary CTA per page
  • · Add trust signals near the CTA
  • · Replace “Submit” → “Get My Results”
Expected lift
+10–25%
Effort
~10 hrs
Trust boost
Reviews,
guarantees
Why this matters

Every extra step or moment of confusion lowers conversion. Simplifying the path delivers immediate lifts in sign-ups and case enquiries.
